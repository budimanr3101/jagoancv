Frequently asked questions

Transparent from the start.

Is the JagoanCV score the same as a company's ATS score?

No. JagoanCV's readiness score is transparent guidance based on structure, completeness, content quality, and consistency. It is not an official Workday, Greenhouse, or other ATS vendor score.

Where are my CV and job description stored?

They are stored locally in the browser on the device you are using. The content is not sent to a JagoanCV backend.

Can I use JagoanCV on my phone?

Yes. On small screens, the editor, A4 preview, and checks appear as three focused views. Because storage is local-first, the document stays in that phone's browser and is not yet synced to other devices.

Can I create documents in Indonesian?

Yes. The interface language and document language can be selected separately, and sample CVs are available in both Indonesian and English.
